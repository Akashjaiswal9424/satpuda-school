Prevention of Sexual Harassment at Workplace Act, 2013

Safe Environment for All

Our school provides a safe, respectful, harassment-free environment for all.

1. Objective

Comply with POSH Act 2013, ensure safe environment for everyone.

2. What Constitutes Harassment

  • Unwelcome physical contact or advances
  • Demand for sexual favors
  • Sexually colored remarks or jokes
  • Showing obscene material

4. Complaint Procedure

  1. Complaint within 3 months of incident
  2. ICC acknowledges within 7 working days
  3. Inquiry completed in 90 days
  4. Strict confidentiality maintained

5. Punishments

  • Written apology and warning
  • Suspension or termination
  • Expulsion of student(s)
  • Filing FIR with police
