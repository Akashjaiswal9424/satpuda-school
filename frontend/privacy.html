Privacy Policy

Last Updated: 1 January 2026

1. Introduction

Satpuda Valley International Public School ("we", "our", "School") is committed to protecting the privacy of our students, parents, and website visitors. This policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information.

2. Information We Collect

  • Personal Information: Name, address, phone, email, DOB, gender, Aadhar (admission only)
  • Student Information: Academic records, attendance, photograph
  • Technical Data: IP address, browser type, cookies

3. How We Use Your Information

  • Process admission enquiries and applications
  • Publish exam results and academic records
  • Send important notices and updates
  • Comply with legal requirements

4. Data Sharing

We do NOT sell or trade your personal information. We share only with MP Board/UDISE, trusted service providers (SMS, WhatsApp, hosting), and when required by law.

5. Children's Privacy

Data collected only through parents/guardians for educational purposes, as per DPDP Act 2023 (India).

6. Cookies

We use cookies to enhance your experience. Manage via browser settings.

7. Data Security

  • HTTPS/SSL encryption
  • Bcrypt password hashing
  • JWT authentication with rate limiting

8. Your Rights

Access, correct, or delete your data. Contact us anytime.
