Anti-Ragging Policy

As per UGC Regulations & Supreme Court Guidelines

Ragging is a CRIMINAL OFFENCE

Strictly prohibited. Offenders face disciplinary action including expulsion and FIR.

1. Definition

Any act — physical, verbal, written, or digital — causing physical/psychological harm, fear, shame, or forcing against will.

2. Zero Tolerance

Our school follows ZERO TOLERANCE. Every incident thoroughly investigated.

3. Anti-Ragging Committee

  • Chairperson / Principal
  • Senior Teacher
  • Parent Representative
  • Local Police Representative

4. Punishments

  1. Written warning & apology
  2. Suspension from school
  3. Withholding of results / TC
  4. Expulsion from school
  5. FIR with local police
