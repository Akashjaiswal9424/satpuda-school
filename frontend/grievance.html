Grievance Redressal Committee

As per RTE Act 2009 & MP Board Guidelines

📢 Purpose

Address complaints from parents, students, staff, and public in a fair and time-bound manner.

🎯 Scope

  • Academic issues (teaching, exams)
  • Fee-related concerns
  • Transport / bus service
  • Behavioral / disciplinary concerns
  • Harassment, ragging, discrimination
  • Infrastructure complaints

📝 How to File Complaint

  1. Submit in writing or email to school office
  2. Mention name, contact, and details
  3. Attach supporting documents
  4. Acknowledgement within 3 working days
  5. Resolution within 15 working days
  6. If unsatisfied, escalate to Chairperson / DEO
